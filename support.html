Support

Quotebook

Write to support@ayoquotethat.com. Reports of abusive content are answered within 24 hours; everything else within a few days.

Common questions

Someone quoted me saying something I never said.
Open the quote and mark it I never said this — it will carry a DENIED badge for everyone. If it is abusive rather than merely wrong, tap ⋯ → Report this quote.
How do I stop someone contacting me?
Tap ⋯ on any of their quotes, or the ⋯ on their profile, and choose Block. Neither of you will see the other, and neither can quote the other. Undo it in Profile → Blocked accounts.
How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete account. It asks for your password and for you to type DELETE, then removes everything permanently.
I forgot my password.
Tap "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in screen. We email you a eight digit code.
Who can see my quotes?
Only the members of the quotegroup you posted them to. See the Privacy Policy.
